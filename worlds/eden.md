# Eden

Home world. Forests, iron, and landing grounds. The owner runs a public outpost here. One of six worlds circling the star Varda.

## Overview

## Compass

North is not derived yet. When it is, it will come from the motion of the sun or of Varda as seen from this world. Bearings are degrees clockwise from that north. On the map, east is +x and north is +y, in meters from the origin anchor.

An unverified relief plate uses a different convention for its own north: the bearing from Talon's Public Outpost to Dragon's Lair. That convention is not derived north, and `north_derived` stays false. The plate is shifted so Dragon's Lair still sits at the display origin. See the note under Points of interest.

## Points of interest

Dragons Lair Base, logged 2026-11-12. TalonDragon000's base. Torch. Outdoor machines under a wooden overhang. Nav showed Power 1d 8h. This base is the display origin for now. The owner may later designate the Eden Outpost instead. To change the origin, set `origin` false here and true on the new place, stored at x 0, y 0.

V3KTR first spawn, the same day. About 276 m from Dragons Lair Base. Bearing unknown, so it is not plotted. Two stripped travel pods. A Workbench. Mixed conifer and broadleaf forest. Tall gray rock spire. Snowy-peaked mountain. Red-dotted berry-like bushes. Nav marker Elara, about 523 m from the spawn. Bearing unknown.

Seen on the route between the base and the spawn, approximate and not placed: pine and birch woodland, ore-looking rocks, and a large stacked rock outcrop. No bearings and no distances.

Estimate (unverified), logged 2026-11-16. A relief plate for this area is `data/eden-lair-relief-estimate.png`. The map draws it on Eden only when **Estimate (unverified)** is checked. It is not surveyed ground, so it is not in `data/terrain.geojson` and it does not clear fog.

The plate's method: hover-survey heights, elevation = 58 m minus the hover reading, with Talon's Public Outpost as the 0 m datum; positions trilaterated from in-game Nav distances; north is the Outpost-to-Lair line, because the game has no compass. The plate measures from the Outpost. Display metres use Dragons Lair Base as (0, 0), so display north = plate north − 2936.2 m.

Talon's Public Outpost is plotted 2936.2 m south of the Lair on that convention, at elevation 0 m. Talon's Copper Site is plotted east of that line, about 3.2 km from the Lair, at an estimated elevation of +14 m. Elara (about 460 m from the Lair area) and Talon's Silica Site (about 2.7 km and about 2.9 km, no bearing) are noted and not placed. The Lair's interpolated height on the plate is not written onto the origin feature.
