# Eden

Home world. Forests, iron, and landing grounds. The owner runs a public outpost here. One of six worlds circling the star Varda.

## Overview

## Compass

North is not derived yet. When it is, it will come from the motion of the sun or of Varda as seen from this world. Bearings are degrees clockwise from that north. On the map, east is +x and north is +y, in meters from the origin anchor.

An unverified relief plate uses a convention for its own north: the bearing from Talon's Public Outpost to Dragon's Lair. That convention is not derived north, and `north_derived` stays false. The display origin is the Outpost, so the plate and the map share one metre frame. See the note under Points of interest.

## Points of interest

Dragons Lair Base, logged 2026-11-12. TalonDragon000's base. Torch. Outdoor machines under a wooden overhang. Nav showed Power 1d 8h. It is not the display origin. On the Outpost-to-Lair convention it is 2800 m due north of Talon's Public Outpost.

V3KTR first spawn, the same day. About 276 m from Dragons Lair Base. Bearing unknown, so it is not plotted. Two stripped travel pods. A Workbench. Mixed conifer and broadleaf forest. Tall gray rock spire. Snowy-peaked mountain. Red-dotted berry-like bushes. Nav marker Elara, about 523 m from the spawn. Bearing unknown.

Seen on the route between the base and the spawn, approximate and not placed: pine and birch woodland, ore-looking rocks, and a large stacked rock outcrop. No bearings and no distances.

Estimate (unverified), logged 2026-11-16. A relief plate for this area is `data/eden-lair-relief-estimate.png`. The map draws it on Eden only when **Estimate (unverified)** is checked. It is not surveyed ground, so it is not in `data/terrain.geojson` and it does not clear fog.

The plate's method: hover-survey heights, elevation = 58 m minus the hover reading, with Talon's Public Outpost as the 0 m datum. The Lair distance is the 2.8 km ground-panorama HUD. Silica and Copper are placed from that same panorama. Their bearings are about plus or minus 15 degrees, and their elevations are unknown. North is the Outpost-to-Lair line, because the game has no compass. Display metres are plate metres. Talon's Public Outpost is (0, 0).

Talon's Public Outpost is the display origin, at elevation 0 m. Dragon's Lair is 2800 m due north. Talon's Silica Site is 149 m northwest. Talon's Copper Site is 543 m WSW. Looking south from the Lair, Copper is to the right of the Outpost. An earlier Copper fix, about 3.5 km away with a height of +14 m, was a different marker and has been removed. Elara, about 460 m from the Lair area, has no bearing and is not placed. The plate's interpolated Lair height is not written onto the Lair feature.
