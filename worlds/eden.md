# Eden

Home world. Forests, iron, and landing grounds. The owner runs a public outpost here. One of six worlds circling the star Varda.

## Overview

## Compass

North is a sun-referenced estimate. Sunrise to sunset is treated as east to west, about plus or minus 15 degrees. Bearings are degrees clockwise from that north. On the map, east is +x and north is +y, in meters from the origin anchor. `north_derived` is true, and the uncertainty is in `north_note`.

The relief plate uses the same frame. Talon's Public Outpost is the display origin. See the note under Points of interest.

## Points of interest

Talon's Public Outpost is the Eden display origin, at 0 m east and 0 m north, drawn as a red star. The offset from Dragons Lair Base is measured: east −2789.3, north 244.

Dragons Lair Base, logged 2026-11-12. TalonDragon000's base. Torch. Outdoor machines under a wooden overhang. Nav showed Power 1d 8h. It is not the display origin, and it uses a normal marker. On the sun-referenced frame it is 2.8 km from Talon's Public Outpost at true azimuth 275 degrees, west and slightly north.

V3KTR first spawn, the same day. About 276 m from Dragons Lair Base. Bearing unknown, so it is not plotted. Two stripped travel pods. A Workbench. Mixed conifer and broadleaf forest. Tall gray rock spire. Snowy-peaked mountain. Red-dotted berry-like bushes. Nav marker Elara, about 523 m from the spawn. Bearing unknown.

Seen on the route between the base and the spawn, approximate and not placed: pine and birch woodland, ore-looking rocks, and a large stacked rock outcrop. No bearings and no distances.

Estimate (unverified), logged 2026-11-16. A relief plate for this area is `data/eden-lair-relief-estimate.png`. The drawing is `data/eden-lair-relief-estimate.svg`. The map draws the PNG on Eden only when **Estimate (unverified)** is checked. It is not surveyed ground, so it is not in `data/terrain.geojson` and it does not clear fog. Ground seen from the Outpost is in [`data/eden/terrain-notes.md`](../data/eden/terrain-notes.md).

The plate's method: hover-survey heights, elevation = 58 m minus the hover reading, with Talon's Public Outpost as the 0 m datum. North is sun-referenced, about plus or minus 15 degrees. The Lair distance is the 2.8 km ground-panorama HUD, at azimuth 275 degrees. Silica is 149 m at azimuth 223 degrees. Copper is 543 m at azimuth 171 degrees. Silica and Copper elevations are unknown. Display metres are plate metres. Talon's Public Outpost is (0, 0).

Talon's Public Outpost is the display origin, drawn as a red star, at elevation 0 m. Dragon's Lair uses a normal marker. An earlier Copper fix, about 3.5 km away with a height of +14 m, was a different marker and has been removed. Elara, about 460 m from the Lair area, has no bearing and is not placed. The plate's interpolated Lair height is not written onto the Lair feature.
