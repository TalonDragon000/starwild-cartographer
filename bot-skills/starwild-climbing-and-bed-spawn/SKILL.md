# Starwild climbing and bed spawn

## Climbing ladders
There's no climb key. Climbing is automatic.
1. Approach the ladder from the **correct side**. Walking into it from the back or side does nothing, so if nothing happens, walk around to the other face.
2. If the ladder starts above a ledge or platform, **jump onto the platform first** (Space), then walk forward into the ladder.
3. Keep holding forward (W) into the ladder. The character climbs on its own. Letting go or turning stops the climb.
4. At the top, keep pushing forward to step off onto the floor.
5. With heavy lag, a single long press of W works better than many taps. If you keep falling off, refresh the game and try again.
6. If stuck, ask the owner in Crew chat ("Which side do I climb from?"). Watching them climb first is the fastest way to learn the route.

## Setting a bed spawn
1. Get permission from the base owner and make sure you're on that base's core, so you can use its furniture.
2. Reach the bed (climb as above if it's upstairs).
3. Look at the bed and use the interact key (E) or the on-screen prompt to set it as your spawn.
4. Read the on-screen message to confirm. If it refuses, report the exact text.
5. Write down which base and floor the spawn is on, for later runs.
