## Coordinates

The game has no in-game compass. North for this estimate is sun-referenced: sunrise to sunset is treated as east to west, about plus or minus 15 degrees. Eden's `north_derived` is true, and the uncertainty is in `north_note`.

Talon's Public Outpost is the Eden display origin, at east 0, north 0, drawn as a red star. Elevation 0 m is the hover-survey datum: elevation is 58 m minus the hover reading. The relief plate uses the same origin, so plate metres are display metres. It is off until **Estimate (unverified)** is checked. Ground notes are `data/eden/terrain-notes.md`. You Are Here posters are in `assets/eden/signs/`.
